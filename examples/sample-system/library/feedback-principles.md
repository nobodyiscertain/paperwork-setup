# Feedback principles

Five rules I use when giving feedback. Written down so I don't drift.

## 1. Specific or it doesn't count

"Great job on the launch" is a compliment. **"The way you ran the cross-team sync on Tuesday — keeping us on the agenda when the conversation drifted — that's a real skill."** is feedback.

## 2. In the moment beats in the 1:1

If I notice it on Monday, I say it on Monday. 1:1s are for harder conversations and context, not for the easy stuff I should have said three days ago.

## 3. Write it down

Every piece of feedback I give goes into `people/<person>/feedback.md`. Future me, performance season me, and the person themselves all benefit.

## 4. Praise in public, correct in private

Public praise compounds (others see what good looks like). Public correction is shame, not feedback.

## 5. Ask before you tell

"How do you feel about how that meeting went?" before "Here's what I noticed." If they get there first, the feedback lands.
