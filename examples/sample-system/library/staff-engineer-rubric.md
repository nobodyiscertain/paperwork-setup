# Staff engineer rubric (draft)

Working draft. Not company doctrine — my own thinking, refined over time. Use as a discussion starter, not a checklist.

## Scope

A senior engineer is responsible for **their team's** problems. A staff engineer is responsible for **the org's** problems within their area of expertise.

Concretely:
- Picks problems that span team boundaries
- Decisions outlast the project they were made in
- Other teams seek them out for input

## Technical judgment

- Can identify which problems are worth solving — and which aren't
- Trades off speed vs. correctness consciously, not by default
- Comfortable saying "I don't know yet, here's how I'd find out"

## Influence

> Influence is when people change behavior because you said something — and you weren't in the room when it happened.

- Writes docs that get cited months later
- Mentors that grow into seniors themselves
- Sets standards the team adopts without coercion

## What I don't weigh

- Years of experience
- Volume of code
- How much they talk in meetings
- Whether they're "the smartest person in the room"

## Open questions

- How do I assess this fairly when the work is invisible by design?
- Where's the line between "staff" and "principal" in our org?
