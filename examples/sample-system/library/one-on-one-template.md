# 1:1 template

The shape I run every 1:1 in. Adjust as needed but never skip the pulse check.

## Pulse (2 min)

How are you. Honestly. Not "fine."

## Their agenda (15 min)

They drive. I ask, I listen. If they didn't bring an agenda I ask:
- What's been on your mind this week?
- Anything you wanted to talk about that didn't make it to my list?

## My agenda (10 min)

Two or three things max. Drawn from `/prep` — never improvised.

## Promises and closeouts (3 min)

- What did I commit to last time? Did I do it?
- What am I committing to this time?
- Write both as `- [ ] ...` in `one-on-ones.md`.

## Wrap (1 min)

"Anything else?" Always asked. Often the most important thing of the meeting.
